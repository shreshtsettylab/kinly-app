# Kinly on Product Hunt: launch kit

Everything to paste into Product Hunt, plus the messages for friends. Product Hunt is at
https://www.producthunt.com/launch. You post it from your own account: you're both the "hunter"
and the "maker", which is normal now and needs no nomination from anyone else.

---

## Fix before launch day

Product Hunt visitors try things straight away and comment in public, so these matter more than usual.

1. **Pricing promises things Kinly doesn't do yet.** Free lists "basic reminders" and Plus lists
   "Advanced goal alignment, reminders and follow-ups". Kinly has no reminders. Remove or reword
   these before launch.
2. **Google Calendar import is capped at 100 people.** That cap stays until Google verifies the app,
   and verification takes a few weeks. Everyone sees the "Google hasn't verified this app" screen
   until then. Submit verification now (docs/GOOGLE_VERIFICATION.md), or launch knowing that a busy
   day could hit the cap.
3. **Email sign-up.** Supabase's built-in email sender only sends a few emails an hour, so on a busy
   day some people won't get their confirmation email. Kinly works without an account, so say
   "no sign-up needed" up front. To fix it properly, set up your own email sender in Supabase, or
   offer Google sign-in only.
4. **Data stays in one browser.** People will ask about syncing between phone and laptop. Answer it
   in the first comment (below), so it comes across as a privacy choice, not a gap.

## Launch day

- **When:** Product Hunt days start at 12:01 AM Pacific, which is **1:31 PM in India** (in winter).
  Schedule the post for then, so it's up for the full 24 hours.
- **Which day:** Tuesday to Thursday get the most visitors and the most competition. Saturday or
  Sunday is quieter and easier to place well.
- **Be there:** reply to every comment through the day; replies count for a lot.
- **Don't ask for upvotes.** Product Hunt's rules say not to, and it lowers the ranking of posts that
  get sudden votes from new accounts. Ask people to *try it and share feedback* instead (the
  messages below do this).
